About Us

Built Around the Guest, Not the Brochure

VIP Yachts operates a curated fleet from Dubai Marina Yacht Club, with one goal: make every charter feel effortless from booking to the moment you step back onto the dock.

Our Story

VIP Yachts began with a simple observation: most yacht rentals in Dubai looked impressive online but fell short on the water — inconsistent crews, vague pricing, and little attention to the details that actually make a charter memorable.

We built our fleet and our booking process around fixing that. Every yacht is maintained to the same standard regardless of tier, every price is shown upfront in AED with no hidden fees, and every crew member is trained to anticipate what a group needs before they have to ask.

Today we operate a fleet spanning Classic, Premium, and Luxury tiers from Dubai Marina Yacht Club, serving everything from two-hour sunset cruises to full overnight charters.
