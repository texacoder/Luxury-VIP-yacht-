Every package includes a licensed captain and crew, fuel, Dubai Marina departure fees, safety equipment, and chilled water and soft drinks onboard.

Payment

We don't take payment through the website. Once you send your charter request via WhatsApp, our team will confirm the details with you directly and arrange payment, either online or in cash at the marina before your charter.
